About the platform

Built by students who have been there

Backlog Buddy started with a simple problem: preparing for a backlog exam means hunting for previous papers across WhatsApp groups, seniors and notice boards, and then guessing which units matter most. This platform puts all of it in one place, organised by branch, semester and subject.

Focused, not exhaustive

We do not try to replace your syllabus or college. We help you find what to study first when time is short.

Honest labelling

Every important question carries a label that describes its history in previous papers. Nothing is presented as a guaranteed exam question.

Community driven

Papers and notes are added through the admin panel, so the library grows as more students contribute material.

Where the demo data comes from

The catalogue currently ships with a generated demo dataset covering nine branches and eight semesters, including papers, questions and study materials, so every screen can be explored end to end. Real papers can replace it at any time through the admin panel.

Responsible use

Our promise about exam questions

  • We analyse the papers available on the platform and count how often a topic appears.
  • We label questions as Very Important, Frequently Asked, Repeated Question, Important Topic or Practice Question based on that history.
  • We never claim that a specific question will appear in your examination.
  • We always recommend covering the complete syllabus and following your college materials.

    • Papers shown on Backlog Buddy are reconstructed for practice from previous examination papers. Always verify the regulation, syllabus and marks scheme with your department before relying on any paper.
